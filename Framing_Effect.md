# Framing Effect

## What is Framing Effect?

**Framing Effect** ek psychological effect hai jisme **same information ko different way mein present karne se**, logon ka perception aur decision change ho sakta hai.

Simple language:

> **Information same hai, lekin presentation change hai → reaction change ho sakta hai.**

---

## Simple Example

Maan lo ek product ka success rate **90%** hai.

### Positive Frame

> ✅ **90% customers successfully use this product.**

### Negative Frame

> ⚠️ **10% customers fail to get the expected result.**

Mathematically:

**90% Success = 10% Failure**

Information same hai, lekin presentation different hai.

---

## Business Example

Suppose ek product ₹5,000 ka hai.

### Frame 1

> **Save ₹1,000 today!**

### Frame 2

> **Pay only ₹4,000 today.**

Dono ka actual price same ho sakta hai, lekin wording customer ki perception ko influence kar sakti hai.

---

## Marketing Example

Instead of:

> ❌ "This product has a 5% failure rate."

A company may say:

> ✅ "95% of customers successfully use this product."

Yahan information ko positive frame mein present kiya gaya hai.

---

## Why Does It Happen?

Hum decisions sirf raw facts ke basis par nahi lete.

Information **kaise present ki gayi hai**, woh bhi perception ko affect kar sakti hai.

### Formula

**Same Information + Different Presentation = Different Perception**

---

## Real-Life Example

Food product:

> **"90% fat-free"**

vs.

> **"Contains 10% fat"**

Dono mathematically same ho sakte hain, lekin first statement product ko comparatively healthier feel kara sakti hai.

---

## Business Use

Framing Effect commonly relevant hai:

- 🛒 Product Marketing
- 💰 Pricing
- 📢 Advertising
- 📊 Presentations
- 🛍️ E-commerce
- 🤝 Sales
- 💼 Business Communication

---

## Remember

> **"It's not always what you say, but how you frame it."**

### Key Idea

**Facts → Frame → Perception → Decision**

---

## Important

Framing Effect ka matlab ye nahi hai ki presentation **hamesha** decision change karegi.

People ki knowledge, preferences, context aur situation bhi decision ko affect karte hain.
