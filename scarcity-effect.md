
# Scarcity Effect

## What is Scarcity Effect?

**Scarcity Effect** ka matlab hai ki jab koi product, opportunity ya resource **limited** available hota hai, toh uski perceived value ya urgency increase ho sakti hai.

### Simple Example

Normal:

> "Shoes available."

Scarcity:

> **"Only 2 pairs left!"**

Second message customer ko jaldi decision lene ke liye motivate kar sakta hai.

### Common Examples

* Only 2 left
* Limited stock
* Sale ends tonight
* Limited edition
* Early-bird offer
* Last few seats

### Why It Works?

Human mind limited resources ko potentially more valuable samajh sakta hai.

Agar koi cheez easily available hai, toh hum usko baad mein lene ka soch sakte hain.

Lekin:

> **"Kal available nahi hoga."**

toh urgency increase ho sakti hai.

### Business Use

E-commerce, events, ticketing, fashion aur limited-edition products mein scarcity messaging commonly dekhi ja sakti hai.

### Remember

> **Limited availability → Higher urgency**

### Important

Fake scarcity trust ko damage kar sakti hai.

Agar website baar-baar "Only 2 left!" dikha rahi hai aur stock actually unlimited hai, toh customer ka trust lose ho sakta hai.
