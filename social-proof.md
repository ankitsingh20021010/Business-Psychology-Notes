
# Social Proof

## What is Social Proof?

**Social Proof** ek psychological phenomenon hai jisme log dusron ke behaviour, opinions ya experiences ko dekhkar decide karte hain ki kya karna chahiye.

Simple language mein:

> **"Agar bahut log ye kar rahe hain, toh shayad ye good choice hai."**

### Examples

Amazon:

> ⭐ 4.8/5
> 25,000+ Reviews

Customer soch sakta hai:

> "Itne logon ne purchase kiya hai, product probably reliable hoga."

### Types of Social Proof

* Customer reviews
* Ratings
* Testimonials
* Number of customers
* "Best Seller"
* Influencer recommendations
* Expert opinions
* User-generated content

### Real-Life Example

Do restaurants hain:

Restaurant A → Empty

Restaurant B → Full

Kuch log naturally Restaurant B ko more trustworthy ya popular assume kar sakte hain.

### Business Use

Website par:

> **10,000+ Customers**

ya

> **4.9★ from 5,000+ Reviews**

social proof provide karta hai.

### Remember

> **People often look at other people's behaviour when deciding what to do.**

### Important

Social proof useful hai, lekin popularity automatically quality prove nahi karti.
