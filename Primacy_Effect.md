# Primacy Effect

## What is Primacy Effect?

**Primacy Effect** ek psychological phenomenon hai jisme kisi list, conversation, presentation ya interaction mein **jo information hum sabse pehle receive karte hain, uska impact baad ki information ke comparison mein zyada ho sakta hai.**

Simple language:

> **"Jo cheez pehle dekhi ya suni, woh mind mein strong impression bana sakti hai."**

---

## Simple Example

Maan lo kisi person ke baare mein tumhe do cheezein batayi jaati hain:

> **Intelligent, hardworking, helpful, careless**

Tumhara first impression mostly positive ho sakta hai, kyunki pehle tumne **"intelligent" aur "hardworking"** suna.

Ab order change kar do:

> **Careless, selfish, helpful, intelligent**

Tumhara initial impression different ho sakta hai.

Yahan **information ka order** important hai.

---

## Real-Life Example

### First Impression

Interview mein candidate starting mein:

> "Hello Sir, nice to meet you. I have worked on 5 projects."

Agar starting interaction strong hai, toh interviewer ke mind mein candidate ka initial impression positive ban sakta hai.

Baad ki information ko woh existing impression ke context mein interpret kar sakta hai.

---

## Marketing Example

Ek website par customer sabse pehle dekhta hai:

> **"Trusted by 50,000+ Customers"**

Uske baad:

- Product features
- Price
- Specifications
- Reviews

First information customer ke mind mein product ke baare mein initial impression create kar sakti hai.

## Primacy Effect vs Recency Effect

Dono ko confuse mat karna.

### Primacy Effect

> **Beginning ki information ka strong impact**

### Recency Effect

> **End mein recently mili information ka strong impact**
