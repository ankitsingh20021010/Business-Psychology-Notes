# Default Effect

## What is Default Effect?

**Default Effect** ek psychological phenomenon hai jisme log aksar **pre-selected ya already chosen option ko change kiye bina accept kar lete hain**.

Simple language:

> **Jo option already selected hai, usko waise hi rehne dena easy lagta hai.**

---

## Simple Example

Maan lo kisi website par subscription plans hain:

- Basic
- Pro ✅ **(Already Selected)**
- Premium

Agar customer ko Pro automatically selected milta hai, toh woh bina option change kiye **Pro plan continue** kar sakta hai.

Yahan **Pro = Default Option** hai.

---

## Real-Life Example

Phone mein:

> **Dark Mode: OFF**

Agar user settings change nahi karta, toh phone default setting par hi rahega.

Similarly:

> **Language: English**

Agar English already selected hai, toh bahut users usko change nahi karenge.

---

## Business Example

Suppose ek SaaS website checkout par:

> ☑️ **Auto-renew subscription**

already selected hai.

Customer agar checkbox ko manually uncheck nahi karta, toh subscription automatically renew ho sakta hai.

Yahan **Auto-renew = Default Option** hai.

---

## Why Does It Happen?

Default option ko choose karne mein:

- Less effort lagta hai
- Decision lene ki zarurat kam hoti hai
- Log assume kar sakte hain ki default option recommended hai
- Changing the option requires an extra action

### Formula

**Pre-selected Option → Less Effort → Higher Chance of Staying With Default**

## Example: Food Ordering

Suppose food app par:

**Combo Meal**

> Burger + Fries + Coke ✅

already selected hai.

Customer agar sirf burger chahta hai, toh usko manually items remove karne padenge.

Kuch customers default combo ko hi continue kar sakte hain.

---

## Default Effect vs Choice

### Without Default

> Choose your plan:
>
> ○ Basic  
> ○ Pro  
> ○ Premium

Customer ko actively decision lena hai.

### With Default

> Choose your plan:
>
> ○ Basic  
> **● Pro (Recommended)**  
> ○ Premium

Ab Pro already selected hai, isliye decision process easier ho sakta hai.

---

## Remember

> **"The easiest option to choose is often the option already selected."**

### Key Idea

**Default → Less Effort → Less Decision-Making → More Likely to Stay**

---

## Important

Default Effect ka matlab ye nahi hai ki **har person default option choose karega**.

Agar user ki strong preference ho, price difference significant ho, ya default option clearly unsuitable ho, toh woh default change kar sakta hai.

---

## Quick Example

> **Free Trial → Auto-renew ON by default**

Customer ko manually **OFF** karna padega.

That's a simple example of the **Default Effect**.
