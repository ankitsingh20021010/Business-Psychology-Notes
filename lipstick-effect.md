# Lipstick Effect

## What is Lipstick Effect?

**Lipstick Effect** ek consumer-behaviour concept hai.

Iska basic idea hai:

> Jab log expensive ya major luxury purchase afford nahi kar pate, toh kabhi-kabhi woh relatively **small aur affordable luxury** par spend karke apni desire for luxury ko satisfy karte hain.

### Simple Example

Maan lo:

* ₹1 Crore ka house → Abhi afford nahi kar sakte ❌
* ₹80,000 ka phone → Afford kar sakte ho ✅

Person expensive purchase nahi kar pa raha, lekin ek premium phone kharidne se usko **luxury/premium feeling** mil sakti hai.

### Other Examples

* Luxury car ❌ → Premium smartwatch ✅
* ₹5 lakh vacation ❌ → ₹15,000 weekend trip ✅
* Designer outfit ❌ → Premium shoes ✅
* Expensive jewellery ❌ → Premium perfume ✅

### Why "Lipstick"?

Term ka famous example lipstick hai.

Economic uncertainty ke time par kuch consumers major luxury items ki jagah relatively inexpensive beauty/luxury products purchase kar sakte hain.

### Business Insight

Companies is behaviour ko samajhkar **affordable luxury products** create karti hain.

Example:

> "Premium experience at a lower price."

### Remember

> **Big luxury afford nahi kar pa rahe → Small luxury se treat karna.**

### Important

Ye universal rule nahi hai. Consumer spending income, prices, economic conditions aur individual circumstances ke according change hoti hai.
