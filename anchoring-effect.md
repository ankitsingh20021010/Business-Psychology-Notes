
# Anchoring Effect

## What is Anchoring Effect?

**Anchoring Effect** ek psychological bias hai jisme hum kisi decision ko lete waqt **pehli information ya pehle number ko reference point (anchor)** bana lete hain.

Baad mein jo information milti hai, hum usko aksar usi first information ke comparison mein judge karte hain.

### Simple Example

Maan lo ek shop par do products hain:

* Product A → ₹5,00,000
* Product B → ₹5,000

₹5,00,000 dekhne ke baad ₹5,000 comparatively **bahut cheap** lag sakta hai.

Yahan **₹5,00,000 anchor** ka kaam kar raha hai.

### Marketing Example

Original Price: ~~₹10,000~~
Sale Price: **₹4,999**

Customer ke mind mein ₹10,000 pehle hi reference point ban gaya.

Isliye ₹4,999 ek attractive deal feel ho sakti hai.

### Real Life

Negotiation mein bhi anchoring use hoti hai.

Agar seller pehle ₹10 lakh demand karta hai aur baad mein ₹8 lakh par agree karta hai, toh buyer ke mind mein ₹10 lakh initial reference point ban chuka hota hai.

### Remember

> **First number can influence how we see the next number.**

### Key Idea

**Anchor → Comparison → Perception**

### Important

Anchoring ka effect har situation mein same nahi hota. Person ki knowledge, context aur available information bhi decision ko affect kar sakti hai.
