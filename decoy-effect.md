
# Decoy Effect

## What is Decoy Effect?

**Decoy Effect** ek psychological effect hai jisme ek **third option** introduce kiya jata hai jo intentionally less attractive hota hai.

Uska purpose aksar kisi doosre option ko comparatively **more attractive** banana hota hai.

### Simple Example

Imagine:

| Plan  | Price |
| ----- | ----: |
| Basic |  ₹100 |
| Pro   |  ₹200 |
| Decoy |  ₹190 |

Agar ₹190 wale option mein Pro ke comparison mein significantly kam benefits hain, toh customer soch sakta hai:

> "Sirf ₹10 extra mein Pro mil raha hai."

Ab **₹200 Pro plan** zyada attractive lagne lagta hai.

### Simple Formula

**Option A + Option B + Decoy → B becomes more attractive**

### Real-Life Example

Cinema:

* Small popcorn → ₹100
* Medium popcorn → ₹180
* Large popcorn → ₹190

Medium aur Large ke beech sirf ₹10 ka difference dekhkar customer Large choose kar sakta hai.

### Remember

> **A third option can change how we compare the first two options.**

### Business Use

Pricing pages mein different plans design karte waqt businesses is concept ka use kar sakte hain.

### Important

Decoy tabhi effective hota hai jab options ka comparison customer ke liye meaningful ho.
